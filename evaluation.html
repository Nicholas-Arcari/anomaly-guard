Evaluation

Detection quality

These figures come from one seed and a threshold set by hand in the settings bar. The honest operating point is set on the legitimate-only calibration split at a target false-positive rate (ADR-009, task D-06), and every rate needs a confidence interval over at least 20 seeds (D-04, D-05). Accuracy is not shown on purpose: the classes are imbalanced.
