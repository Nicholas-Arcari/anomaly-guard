Statistical models

The model learns what normal input looks like for each endpoint and parameter, from the legitimate training split only — attacks never enter training (ADR-004). Each signal turns how unusual a value is into a probability p and a score s = −log₁₀ p (ADR-007); the request's score is the highest of them.
